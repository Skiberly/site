How ordering works

  1. Send a request

    Fill in the commission form or DM me with your character's references.

  2. Pay in full

    100% prepayment through Boosty. After that you join the queue.

  3. Wait your turn

    One piece takes from one week to two months. Your spot is always visible on Trello.

  4. Sketch, then final

    Changes cost extra: $15 at the sketch stage, $30 on a nearly finished piece.

Terms of service

By ordering a commission you confirm that you have read these terms and agree with them. I can cancel an order if the terms are broken.

  1. 100% prepayment. You join the queue once the payment is made. I can't refund a paid order if you change your mind: this depends on the payment services I work with, not on me. Please keep it in mind before ordering.
  2. No deadlines. Commissions are done in queue order, and one piece takes from one week to two months. If you need it by a specific date, the price goes up by 30%.
  3. Changes are paid. Each change costs $15 at the sketch stage and $30 on a nearly finished illustration.
  4. Private pieces cost +50%. Tell me before ordering if you want the drawing to stay private. Otherwise it will be published.
  5. Portfolio use. I may use any illustration in my portfolio, print publications, videos and so on. This doesn't apply to private commissions. I will never recolor your commission for someone else: it's yours.
